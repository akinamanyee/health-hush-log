# M14 build plan — 私隱與資料使用更明確

## Intent
Users see what stays local and what may temporarily leave the device at the moment they use the app.

## Build
- Create one reusable privacy notice with cover, dashboard, module and summary wording.
- State that health records stay on this device, photos are sent only for reading, summaries send grade labels only, and the app is not a medical diagnosis.
- Replace scattered footer copy with the shared notice.

## Verify
- Cover, dashboard, all four module pages and summary show the relevant privacy/data wording.
